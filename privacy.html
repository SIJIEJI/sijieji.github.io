Website Privacy

Updated November 8, 2026

The homepage, Research Overview and AI for Bio pages use Umami Cloud for basic visitor analytics: page visits, approximate country/region/city, referral sources and browser/device information.

This analytics service does not use cookies. It is not configured with names, email addresses, user accounts, session recordings or advertising profiles. Approximate cities are inferred from network information and may be inaccurate, especially with VPNs or shared networks. A city does not identify a person or institution.

This site's tracking configuration excludes query strings and fragments from page URLs and honors the Do Not Track setting where a browser exposes it. Browser content blockers can also prevent analytics from loading; the site remains usable without it.

Reports are private and are not publicly shared. Analytics is hosted in Umami Cloud's United States region on a plan with six-month data retention. See Umami's privacy policy for its processing practices.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and other embedded resources may process network requests independently of this analytics service. See GitHub's privacy statement.
