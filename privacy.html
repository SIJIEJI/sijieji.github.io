Website Privacy Notice

Updated November 8, 2026

This notice covers optional Google Analytics on the homepage, Research Overview and AI for Bio pages at sijieji.github.io.

Optional visitor analytics

If you select "Accept analytics", this site loads Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits, approximate location (including city), referral sources and basic browser/device information. Google derives approximate locations from network information; these locations may be inaccurate. These statistics do not identify a visitor by name or establish their institutional affiliation.

Google Analytics uses cookies such as _ga to distinguish browsers and sessions. This site's configuration disables Google signals and advertising personalization signals, and omits query strings and fragments from page URLs sent by its tracking code. Automatic enhanced measurement is disabled.

Your choice

No Google Analytics script is loaded until you accept. You may reject analytics and continue using the site. Your preference is stored in your browser's local storage. Select "Analytics settings" on any of the three pages to change your choice. Rejecting after acceptance disables further analytics collection on that page and removes the site's Google Analytics cookies; it does not delete information already sent.

Service providers and access

Google processes analytics data to provide the service. Analytics reports are accessed through a private Google Analytics account and are not published on this website. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and review Google's Privacy Policy.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting and other resources embedded in individual pages may make their own network requests independently of optional analytics. See GitHub's privacy statement.
